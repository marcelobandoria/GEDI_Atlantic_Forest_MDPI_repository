# Static assets guide

The repository workflow generates the statistical and data-dependent figures. Some manuscript figures are cartographic, visual-interpretation, or diagrammatic products and are not regenerated by the R statistical workflow.

## Required static figures

Place the final manuscript versions of Figures 1-6 under:

```text
manuscript_assets/figures/
```

Expected filenames:

| Manuscript figure | Expected filename | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Figure 1 | `figure_01_atlantic_forest_extent_old_growth_candidates.png` | Atlantic Forest extent and old-growth candidate forests. |
| Figure 2 | `figure_02_study_area_phytophysiognomies.png` | Study area and distribution of analyzed phytophysiognomies. |
| Figure 3 | `figure_03_sampling_distribution_old_growth_forests.png` | Distribution of sampling across old-growth forests. |
| Figure 4 | `figure_04_visual_interpretation_key.png` | Visual interpretation keys for QA/QC. |
| Figure 5 | `figure_05_gedi_sampling_within_physiognomies.png` | GEDI sampling within old-growth physiognomies. |
| Figure 6 | `figure_06_research_flowchart.png` | Research workflow or flowchart. |

## Recommended formats

Use high-resolution PNG or TIFF files for archived manuscript figures. Keep a consistent naming convention and avoid spaces in filenames.

## Notes

Figure 7 and Supplementary Figures S1-S2 are generated by scripts and should not be manually edited after workflow execution.
